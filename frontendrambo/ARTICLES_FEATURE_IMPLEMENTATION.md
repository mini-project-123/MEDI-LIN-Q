# Articles Feature Implementation Summary

## ✅ **Universal Articles Feature Created**

### 🎯 **Available to All User Types**

The Articles feature is now accessible to **all users** - patients, doctors, and admins - with role-appropriate functionality:

#### **👨‍⚕️ Doctor Access**

- **Sidebar Navigation**: "Articles" tab in doctor dashboard
- **Full Publishing Rights**: Can write and publish medical articles
- **Professional Content**: Share medical knowledge and research

#### **👤 Patient Access**

- **Tab Navigation**: "Articles" tab in patient dashboard
- **Read & Share**: Access all published articles
- **Patient Stories**: Can publish personal health experiences
- **Community Engagement**: Like, comment, and share articles

#### **🏥 Admin Access**

- **Tab Navigation**: "Articles" tab in admin dashboard
- **Content Management**: Oversee all published content
- **Healthcare Insights**: Publish administrative and policy articles
- **Platform Moderation**: Manage community content

### 🚀 **Core Features**

#### **📖 Article Discovery**

- **All Articles Tab**: Browse all published content from the community
- **My Articles Tab**: Manage your own published articles
- **Search Functionality**: Find articles by title, content, or tags
- **Category Filters**: Filter by medical specialties and topics
- **Featured Articles**: Highlighted quality content

#### **✍️ Article Publishing**

- **Rich Editor**: Create comprehensive articles with formatting
- **Categories**: Cardiology, Mental Health, Patient Stories, Healthcare Tech, etc.
- **Tagging System**: Add relevant tags for discoverability
- **Summary Field**: Brief article descriptions for better engagement
- **Instant Publishing**: Articles go live immediately

#### **👥 Social Features**

- **Author Profiles**: See article author's role and specialization
- **Engagement Metrics**: Views, likes, comments, and shares
- **Reading Time**: Estimated time to read each article
- **Interactive Actions**: Like, share, and comment on articles
